ISSUE 1: Guideline 2.1 - In-App Purchase Visibility

Clarification: Forava uses a CREDIT-BASED purchase model, not subscriptions.

We have removed the "Monthly Unlimited" and "Annual Unlimited" subscription products from App Store Connect as they are not implemented in the current version. The app offers consumable credit packs for AI image regeneration.

| credits50          | Family Pack        | $34.99 |
| credits100         | Festival Pack      | $59.99 |

HOW TO ACCESS IN-APP PURCHASES:

Path 1 - Via Settings:
1. Launch app and complete onboarding (select any culture)
2. Tap the gear icon (Settings) in the top-right corner
3. Tap "Buy Regeneration Credits" button
4. All 5 credit packs are displayed for purchase

Path 2 - Via Natural Flow:
1. Complete onboarding and select a cultural event
2. Use all 3 free AI generations
3. Paywall appears automatically showing credit packs

Path 3 - Direct Access:
1. From Welcome screen, tap "Purchase Credits Now" button
2. Paywall displays with all credit pack options

All products are configured for sandbox testing with the Paid Apps Agreement active.
