# Apple App Store Review Guidelines - Summary

Source: https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/

Last Updated: November 15, 2025

---

## Overview

The Apple App Store Review Guidelines comprise five main sections designed to ensure app safety, performance, business practices, design quality, and legal compliance.

## 1. Safety

Apps must avoid objectionable content including defamatory material, violence, sexual content, and harmful misinformation. User-generated content requires moderation systems and abuse reporting mechanisms. Kids Category apps need parental controls and cannot include third-party analytics collecting identifiable information. Medical apps face scrutiny regarding accuracy claims, and apps cannot encourage dangerous behaviors like drunk driving or drug use.

**Key Requirements:**
- No objectionable content (defamatory, violent, sexual, harmful misinformation)
- User-generated content must have moderation and reporting systems
- Kids Category apps require parental controls
- No third-party analytics collecting identifiable information in Kids apps
- Medical apps must have accurate claims
- Cannot encourage dangerous behaviors (drunk driving, drug use)

## 2. Performance

Apps must be complete, functional, and tested before submission. Metadata including descriptions and screenshots must accurately reflect functionality. Hardware compatibility requirements specify that iPhone apps should run on iPad when possible. Apps cannot drain battery excessively or perform unrelated background processes like cryptocurrency mining.

**Key Requirements:**
- Apps must be complete and functional before submission
- Metadata must accurately reflect app functionality
- iPhone apps should run on iPad when possible
- Cannot drain battery excessively
- No unrelated background processes (e.g., cryptocurrency mining)

## 3. Business

Developers must use in-app purchase for digital content and features. Auto-renewable subscriptions require clear disclosures and must provide ongoing value lasting at least seven days across devices. Apps cannot manipulate ratings or engage in fraudulent practices. Various purchase method exceptions exist for reader apps, physical goods, and person-to-person services.

**Key Requirements:**
- Must use in-app purchase for digital content and features
- Auto-renewable subscriptions:
  - Require clear disclosures
  - Must provide ongoing value (minimum 7 days)
  - Must work across devices
- Cannot manipulate ratings or engage in fraud
- Exceptions exist for:
  - Reader apps
  - Physical goods
  - Person-to-person services

## 4. Design

Apps require unique, substantial functionality—not merely repackaged websites or catalogs. Copycat apps are rejected. Extensions must comply with specific programming guides. Login services using third-party providers must offer equivalent alternatives respecting user privacy.

**Key Requirements:**
- Must have unique, substantial functionality
- Not just repackaged websites or catalogs
- No copycat apps
- Extensions must comply with programming guides
- Third-party login must offer privacy-respecting alternatives

## 5. Legal

Privacy protection is paramount. Apps must disclose data collection practices and obtain explicit user consent. Health data receives special protection and cannot be used for marketing. Intellectual property must be properly licensed. Gaming and gambling apps require appropriate licensing and geo-restriction.

**Key Requirements:**
- Privacy protection is paramount
- Must disclose data collection practices
- Must obtain explicit user consent
- Health data:
  - Special protection required
  - Cannot be used for marketing
- Intellectual property must be properly licensed
- Gaming and gambling apps:
  - Require appropriate licensing
  - Must implement geo-restrictions
