# Forava — Brand Kit

## Brand Essence
**Forava** = *Forever + Ava* (life/breath): an enduring, human connection that lasts through ups and downs.

## Colors
- **Forava Indigo**: #3E3A9F (primary)
- **Warm Coral**: #FF6A6A (accent)
- **Sun Glow**: #FFD166 (support)
- **Deep Charcoal**: #1E1E24 (text)
- **Cloud White**: #F8F9FB (background)

## Typography
- **Display / Logo**: *SF Pro Rounded* or *Nunito* (rounded, human, friendly)
- **UI Text**: *SF Pro* (system), fallback: *Inter*

## Voice & Tone
Warm, inclusive, and reassuring. Celebrates bonds across all cultures.

## Icon
A resilient, continuous loop forming a subtle heart‑like shape with an inner “F” motif.
